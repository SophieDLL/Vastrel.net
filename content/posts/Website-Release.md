## Welcome to Vastrel.net




Welcome to the official Vastrel.Net website. This is the website created for the Vastrel Group. 




Vastrel is a community within the Minecraft scene, formerly known for our work within 2B2T and most recently within 6B6T. 




The website is planned to remain up for the entire lifespan of Vastrel, considering that Null started in 2015, which should be a long-standing period of time. 




---




## Our Projects and Future Plans




At the current time, the only project that Vastrel will be working on is the 6B6T Archive project.




The plan for this project is to archive 1M^2 in all dimensions within the Minecraft world on 6B6T, making this the LARGEST WORLD DOWNLOAD IN MINECRAFT HISTORY.




---




## Open-source projects




We have decided to fully open source the website for Vastrel.net to make it a little bit of a standing ground for how group and individual sandin could update, work on or create their own world download projects or anything else that they may wish to do. 




Vastrel.net uses [Astro](https://astro.build/)




The theme that we use is called [Astro Cactus](https://github.com/chrismwilliams/astro-theme-cactus), created by Chris Williams.
